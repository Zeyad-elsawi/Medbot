# Clinical Guidelines: Pneumonia

## Diagnosis Criteria
- Chest X-ray showing new infiltrate or consolidation
- Fever > 38°C or hypothermia < 36°C
- Productive cough, dyspnea, or pleuritic chest pain
- Leukocytosis > 10,000/µL or leukopenia < 4,000/µL

## Severity Assessment
Use PSI (Pneumonia Severity Index) or CURB-65 score:
- CURB-65 0-1: Outpatient treatment
- CURB-65 2: Consider hospitalization
- CURB-65 3+: Hospitalize, consider ICU

## First-Line Treatment
### Community-Acquired (mild-moderate)
- Amoxicillin 500mg TDS for 5 days
- OR Doxycycline 200mg loading then 100mg BD

### Community-Acquired (severe / hospitalized)
- Beta-lactam + macrolide combination
- Co-amoxiclav 1.2g IV TDS + Clarithromycin 500mg BD

## Monitoring
- Repeat chest X-ray at 6 weeks to confirm resolution
- Blood cultures before antibiotics if hospitalized
- Oxygen saturation monitoring

## Red Flags (Escalate Immediately)
- SpO2 < 92% on room air
- RR > 30 breaths/min
- Systolic BP < 90 mmHg
- Confusion or altered consciousness

## Source
Based on BTS Guidelines for Community Acquired Pneumonia (2009, updated 2019)
